About

I graduated in 2025 with a B.Tech in Computer Science from Pillai College of Engineering, and I've spent the time since figuring out how the two halves of software actually meet: the code, and the infrastructure it runs on. At TCS, that means patching, performance-testing and debugging real production applications. Before that, an internship at TCiA Global had me provisioning AWS and Azure resources and managing client websites — my first real taste of infrastructure other people actually depended on.

Outside of work I'm usually building something smaller and slightly stranger — a virtual mouse that reads hand gestures through a webcam was one of my favourite college projects. Lately I've also been poking around networking fundamentals and the basics of ethical hacking, mostly to understand systems from the other side.
